# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js + Tailwind CSS (static export). Hosted on GitHub Pages / Vercel.

## Users

Business owners and marketers who need a video editor for ads, VSLs, or brand content. They are evaluating options, have a budget, and need someone trustworthy who can deliver results — fast.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio/landing page that establishes Mat as the go-to video editor for anyone serious about paid traffic. The page demonstrates capability through work samples, builds credibility through results metrics and testimonials, and drives the next step: starting a conversation via email or social media.

## Positioning

Premium freelance video editor with a track record of 500M+ paid views. Not the cheapest option — the one who knows what sells and delivers accordingly.

## Operating Context

- Landing page visited via social media ads (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok links) or direct search
- Visitors are scrolling on mobile or desktop, evaluating within seconds
- No login, no app — single static page, zero friction
- Primary CTAs: email contact or follow/link social media

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no CMS — all content in code until real assets are uploaded
- Images and videos served from `public/assets/` — drop files in, update `lib/data.ts`
- Showreel at hero, portfolio grid (filterable by Ads / VSLs), testimonials + results screenshots in "Not Just Talk" section
- Email is primary conversion; social links secondary
- No e-commerce, no booking system — contact is email only until a form is added

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mat Vision Videos
- Logo: play/triangle icon in blue (#2f9dff) + wordmark
- Accent color: bright blue #2f9dff
- Fonts: Helvetica Neue (all text)
- Tone: confident, direct, no fluff

- Placeholder client logos in logo strip (ExtraSeed, orbio, AMERICOR, matsdto, derila ergo, Paid It Media)

- Real assets to live in `public/assets/` — paths referenced in `lib/data.ts`
- Stats: 500M+ paid views, 100+ clients, 1000+ projects, 8+ years experience

## Product Principles

1. **Credibility first** — work and numbers before marketing speak
2. **Zero friction conversion** — one clear next step, no distractions
3. **Speed to trust** — show results, then ask for the email
4. **Mobile-first** — social traffic is almost always mobile
5. **Upgrade path** — replace placeholders one by one without redesigning
